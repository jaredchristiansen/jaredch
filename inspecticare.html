01 Context

A real gap,
in a neglected market.

Health inspections in Massachusetts were almost entirely paper-based. Inspectors worked from memory, handwriting reports in the field and filing physical documents. There was one digital alternative — and it was widely considered too difficult to use to be worth it.

Problem & Solution
The Problem

Inspectors relied on memory to recall codes, procedural steps, and formatting requirements. Records were inconsistently formatted, hard to retrieve, and frequently stored physically — creating administrative burden and long-term tracking challenges.

New inspectors faced a steep learning curve with no structured support. The existing digital tool was so difficult to use that many preferred paper.

The Opportunity

Rather than simply digitizing paperwork, the insight was simpler: what if inspections were guided instead of memorized?

Inspecticare replaces paper inspections with a structured, step-by-step digital workflow that embeds health codes and required inputs directly — shifting cognitive load away from the inspector and into the product.

"The problem wasn't that inspectors didn't know their job. It was that the tools made doing it well unnecessarily hard."

The insight that started the company

03 The Product

Guided workflow —
not a digital form.

The interface was designed as a structured walkthrough rather than a static form. Each section breaks inspections into clear, digestible steps — reducing reliance on memory and minimizing the risk of missed information.

The guided report flow — step-by-step structure that replaces the need to memorize codes or formatting requirements.

By embedding health codes and required inputs directly into the workflow, the system shifts cognitive load away from the inspector and into the product. New inspectors can complete a compliant report on their first day.

Brand system — built from scratch to balance modern usability with the credibility municipal buyers expect.

The visual system needed to feel modern without appearing informal. For municipalities, credibility matters as much as usability — the brand had to earn trust before a single screen was opened.

04 Development

From guided input
to official record.

The structured workflow feeds directly into dynamically generated PDF reports. A custom JavaScript plugin was developed to transform inspection data into compliant, standardized documentation automatically.

Platform .bubble

The full application was built in Bubble.io — chosen for its speed of iteration and ability to handle complex relational data without a dedicated engineering team. Every workflow, database schema, and permission model was designed and built by hand.

Custom Extension JavaScript

Bubble's native PDF tools couldn't meet municipal compliance standards. I wrote a custom JS plugin to dynamically generate compliant, formatted reports directly from inspection data — translating structured inputs into official documentation automatically.

The tech stack — Bubble.io extended with a custom JavaScript plugin for PDF generation.

→

Why Bubble was the right call

Building solo means making smart choices about where to spend time. Bubble handled auth, the database, and UI rendering — freeing me to focus custom code on the one thing Bubble couldn't do: generating documentation that would hold up in a municipal workflow. That's the tradeoff that made the product shippable.

05 Outcomes

Real municipalities,
real inspections.

Inspecticare is live and in use. Municipalities in Massachusetts are running inspections through the platform — not as a pilot, but as their primary workflow.

What changed for inspectors
Before

Inspectors worked from memory on paper forms. New hires needed significant time before they could work independently. Reports were inconsistently formatted and filed physically.

Retrieving historical records was slow and unreliable — a real problem when violations needed to be referenced across inspections.

After

A new inspector can complete a compliant report on their first day. Every report is consistently formatted, automatically stored, and immediately searchable.

Municipalities have improved documentation reliability, reduced onboarding time for new inspectors, and moved long-term record management out of physical filing systems entirely.

Thousands of inspections completed

The platform has processed thousands of inspections across Massachusetts municipalities — proof that the product works in real field conditions, not just in a prototype environment.

→

Inspecticare isn't a concept or a case study built for a portfolio. It's a live product used by real inspectors, in real field conditions, generating real documentation. That distinction matters — and it's what this kind of early-stage, zero-to-one work is actually for.
